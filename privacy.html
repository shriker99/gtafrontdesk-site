Privacy policy

Last updated Oct 9, 2026

This policy explains how GTA Front Desk handles personal information. GTA Front Desk is operated by XPLOREFUTURE, an Ontario sole proprietorship. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we do

GTA Front Desk answers phone calls for HVAC and plumbing shops in the Greater Toronto Area using an AI virtual assistant. Callers are told they are speaking with a virtual assistant and that the call may be recorded.

What we collect from callers

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Service address
  • A description of the problem or request
  • Call recordings and transcripts

Why we collect it

We use caller information to take a message and pass it to the shop the caller was trying to reach, so the shop can call back. We also use recordings and transcripts to check that the service is working properly and to improve it. We collect only what is needed for this. We do not sell personal information.

What we collect from shops (our customers)

Business name, contact name, email, phone number, business hours and call-handling instructions. Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store full card numbers.

Who processes it for us

  • Voice AI provider (currently Retell AI): answers, records and transcribes calls.
  • Stripe: processes customer payments.
  • Email providers: deliver call summaries and messages.

These providers may store or process data outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of those countries.

How long we keep it

We keep call recordings for no longer than 90 days, unless the shop asks us to keep them for a different period. Messages sent to a shop are that shop's records, and the shop is responsible for how it uses them. Details from website enquiries are kept for up to 12 months unless you become a customer.

Your rights: access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@gtafrontdesk.ca with "privacy" in the subject. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
