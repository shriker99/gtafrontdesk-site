What it handles

Answers 24/7

Greets callers in your company's name and takes their name, address, callback number and what's wrong, then reads it back. Callers are told it's a virtual assistant.

Emergencies flagged first

For a gas smell or a carbon monoxide alarm, callers are told to get out and call 911 or the gas utility first. Gas, carbon monoxide, floods and burst pipes are flagged urgent and emailed to your on-call contact right away; no heat with someone vulnerable at home is flagged urgent and emailed within minutes.

Urgent calls emailed

When a call is urgent, your on-call contact gets an email with what's going on and the number to call back. The desk never promises a time; it tells callers your team will call to confirm.

Booking requests, not guesses

It takes the job request and your team confirms the time. It never invents a slot or quotes a price you haven't approved.

Questions

Does it replace my dispatcher or office staff?

No. It covers after-hours, overflow and the calls nobody gets to. You choose which calls it takes.

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. You set your line to forward unanswered or after-hours calls to the desk. Turn forwarding off any time.

What counts as an emergency?

You set the list. The default is a gas smell, a carbon monoxide alarm, active leaks or flooding, burst pipes, and no heat when someone vulnerable is at home. Those are flagged urgent and emailed to your on-call contact: right away for gas, carbon monoxide, floods and burst pipes, and within minutes for no heat. None of them is treated as routine.

What about privacy and Canadian law?

We handle caller information under Canada's private-sector privacy law (PIPEDA) and only use it to serve your customers. Callers are told they're speaking with a virtual assistant and that calls may be recorded. Your customer list stays yours. We never sell it or use it for other shops. Some of the software we use may store data outside Canada; we'll tell you which providers before you sign up. If text features are added for your shop later, they will follow Canada's anti-spam law (CASL), including opt-outs.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's month-to-month with no long-term contract. Email us to cancel. The full terms are in the short service agreement you see before paying.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few business days after we have your hours, service area and on-call contacts. You test it before it goes live.

Will it sound robotic?

Judge for yourself. The demo is a live call to a sample heating company, so you hear exactly what your customers would hear.
